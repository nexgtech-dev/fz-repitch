Why this belongs on fonezone.me

Gulf buyers message before they buy — especially used devices. Your floating WhatsApp button and warranty-claims process already prove the channel works; this just moves it two steps earlier, where it saves a sale instead of rescuing one.

1

Buyer taps the PDP, thread opens prefilled

The "Ask on WhatsApp" button carries the product context — model, storage, the condition they were viewing. No "hello? is this available?" from scratch.

2

Honest condition answer + real photos

Agent replies in plain language — "Good means micro-marks on the frame, screen glass is clean" — and sends photos of a current Good unit. Honesty here kills the return that a vague "excellent condition!" creates.

3

Battery, delivery, instalments — same thread

Cycles, health %, Riyadh delivery ETA, Tabby in four interest-free payments. The three questions every refurbished buyer asks, answered once, reusable for the next buyer too.

4

Close in the thread, hold the unit

Product link + "reply BUY and I'll hold this unit for 2 hours." A hold beats an abandoned cart, and the conversation history becomes the order record.
