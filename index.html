1

Saudi store, Indian rupees — with piasters

Open fonezone.me from India — as your own team does — and the "Saudi Arabia" store prices everything in ₹122,022.15 INR.

What we found

The currency switcher auto-selects INR for India IPs, then converts keeping two decimals — so every card reads like a currency-conversion bug. Even in SAR, many prices carry piasters: SAR 750.67, SAR 4,025.37, while others sit clean at SAR 4,872.00. The switcher itself offers 170+ currencies — Bitcoin, North Korean Won, Afghan Afghani — with Saudi Riyal buried mid-list.

rendered: "₹ 122,022.15 INR" · "₹ 79,369.49 INR" · "₹ 44,781.53 INR" (every card)
SAR view: "750.67 SAR - 1,275.39 SAR" · "4,025.37 SAR" vs clean "4,872.00 SAR"
switcher options: 170+ incl. "Bitcoin", "North Korean Won"; SAR not default-curated

Why it costs sales

A Gulf shopper who lands on INR — or sees piasters next to riyals — reads "this store can't decide what currency it's in." For a refurbished buyer about to hand over SAR 1,000+ for a used phone, arithmetic hygiene is trust hygiene.

The fix

Round every price to whole riyals. Curate the switcher to three currencies max (SAR default, AED, INR for the diaspora). Auto-select by market, not IP guesswork. On the concept PDP we rebuilt: SAR 750, no piasters, one currency story.

The Saudi store keeps wearing UAE clothes

Support number in +971. A shipping-delay notice about the UAE. On a storefront whose own title tag says "Best Refurbished Mobile Phone in Saudi Arabia."

What we found

The header and footer list Customer Support as +971 565069120 — a UAE number — on the KSA storefront. The announcement bar warns of "current geopolitical conditions in the UAE" shipping delays. A second notice says Tabby is "currently unavailable" due to technical issues — a live payment rail announced as down, on every page.

header/footer: "Customer Support +971 565069120"
announcement: "Due to current geopolitical conditions in the UAE, shipping and service timelines may experience temporary delays"
announcement: "Due to some technical issues, Tabby is currently unavailable"

Why it costs sales

A Riyadh buyer seeing a UAE number and UAE war-risk language asks a simple question: "who am I actually buying from, and where does this ship from?" Uncertainty at the support-line stage becomes refused deliveries later.

The fix

KSA support number on the KSA store (and keep the KSA number on WhatsApp — your warranty process already directs claims there). UAE notices belong on the UAE property or geofenced by market. Tabby/Tamara status should be dynamic, not a frozen apology.

3

Product links that don't match the product

A shared link says 128GB. The page opens on 64GB. The specs table never changes. Whoever clicks that link from Google or WhatsApp gets a different phone than the one promised.

What we found

The product URL apple-iphone-12-128gb-grade loads with Storage = 64GB pre-selected. The specifications table hardcodes "Internal storage — 64GB" regardless of which variant the buyer picks. The SKU field dumps two internal codes: "FMMRAP1264BCGO, 010113-060101". And there's no review or rating widget on a refurbished PDP — zero social proof where the hesitation is highest.

url: /products/apple-iphone-12-128gb-grade
checked: Storage=64GB, Color=Black, Condition=Good
specs: "Internal storage  64GB" (static, ignores 128/256 selection)
sku: "FMMRAP1264BCGO, 010113-060101"

Why it costs sales

Every SEO page, WhatsApp share, and paid ad deep-links into a variant. If the link lands on the wrong storage, you either lose the sale instantly — or win it and create a return. Both outcomes are worse than the fix.

The fix

Variant-aware deep links (URL opens the variant it names), spec tables driven by variant data, one clean SKU, and a review widget fed by your post-delivery WhatsApp ask. The concept PDP shows the iPhone 12 done the way we'd ship it — 128GB pre-selected, specs that follow the variant, whole-riyal pricing.
