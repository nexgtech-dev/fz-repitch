What just happened, and why it pays for itself

Most COD RTO isn't fraud — it's typos, guest-checkout fake numbers, and "I didn't order this" at the door. A 30-second confirmation catches the cheap mistakes before a parcel crosses three states.

1

Order lands, message fires instantly

The moment a COD order is placed, WhatsApp sends the summary. Customer sees the exact model, grade and price they committed to — no surprises at the door.

2

Address shown back, one tap to confirm

Quick-reply buttons, not a form. "This is correct" or "Edit address". Typing a full address on WhatsApp is why most verification flows fail — taps don't.

3

Confirmed → order ships next day

Unconfirmed after 24 hrs? Order pauses with a human follow-up call, not an automatic cancel. The customer stays in control; the parcel doesn't move blind.

4

Optional: ₹1,000 UPI advance, same thread

For higher-value COD, the partial advance link lives in the same chat. One thread from order to delivery tracking — no email, no separate dashboard.
